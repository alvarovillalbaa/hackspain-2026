# Professional Roast Example

## Request

“Roast me about this quarter. I announced six priorities, completed two, and changed the team roadmap four times without writing down why.”

## Example response

### The roast

You did not set a strategy; you released a six-item trailer and cancelled the season halfway through.

### Receipts

- Fact: the user reports announcing six priorities and completing two this quarter.
- Fact: the user reports four roadmap changes without a written rationale.
- Inference: weak change communication, rather than change itself, is likely creating avoidable churn. Team impact is not evaluated because no team evidence was provided.

### What it is costing

People cannot distinguish a considered pivot from the priority of the week, so every new direction carries an avoidable credibility tax.

### Do this now

1. Publish a one-page decision log for the four changes by Friday; measure completion and whether owners can name the current top two priorities.
2. Cap the next cycle at two priorities; add a third only by explicitly removing one and documenting the tradeoff.

The example targets decisions and process, preserves evidence labels, and ends with actions rather than an insult.
